## Goal

Parse and validate X.509 chains and flag weak configurations. Build it end to end, keep everything local and isolated, and finish with something you can demo and explain.

## Prerequisites

- A working Kali box and the CyberForge lab platform (for any hosted targets).
- Comfort with the relevant Manual chapters — link them from your notes as you go.

## Milestones

1. Scope and design — write one paragraph on what 'done' means and sketch the architecture.
2. Minimum viable version — the smallest thing that works end to end.
3. Hardening and depth — handle errors, edge cases and the security properties that matter.
4. Validation — test against the lab, capture evidence, and confirm the security goal is met.
5. Write-up — document what you built, what you learned, and what you would do next.

## Stretch goals

- Add automated tests or a CI check.
- Add a defensive counterpart (detection for what you built).

## Deliverables

- Working code/config in a repo.
- A short report with diagrams and evidence.
- A mapping to ATT&CK/OWASP/CWE where relevant.
